Privacy Policy

Video Silent Export

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Video Silent Export is a Windows app for removing audio from video files quickly and easily. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

What the app accesses

The app only accesses files and folders that you explicitly choose, such as files you add through the file picker, folder picker, or drag-and-drop. It does not scan your device or access files unless you select them in the app.

How your data is processed

Audio removal and video export are performed locally on your device using a bundled copy of FFmpeg. Your video files are not uploaded to any server for processing.

Internet access

Video Silent Export works offline and does not require an account or internet connection to use its core features.

Data collection

I do not collect personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, filenames, file contents, folder paths, media metadata, or tracking data through Video Silent Export.

Sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Local storage

The app may store local preferences on your device, such as output folder, export format, filename style, or other app preferences. This information remains on your device.

Your control

  • You choose which files and folders the app can access.
  • You choose where exported silent videos are saved.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.
  • You can remove local app data by deleting the app and its local preferences from your device.
