CatHunt: Paw & Play

Help for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Contact support

For help, feedback, or feature requests, email mykamel@gmail.com. Include your device model, system version, CatHunt version, and a description of the issue. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

Getting started

Aquarium is free. Choose a mode on the home screen to play. Paid modes offer a 15-second preview. Hold the leave control for two seconds to return to the home screen.

Purchases and restoring access

Open Unlock modes to view the available one-time purchases. The Fly & Bugs Pack unlocks Fly. The Mouse & Lizard Pack unlocks Mouse and Lizard. If you previously purchased a pack, use Restore Purchases in the Owner shop while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Sound and haptics

Sound and iPhone haptics can be changed on the home screen. Haptic feedback requires a supported iPhone; it is not available on iPad or Mac.

Privacy

CatHunt has no account requirement or ads. Read the CatHunt Privacy Policy for details about local settings and Apple in-app purchases.
